A text

The speeds are how many times faster than real time the clock's imaginary time runs. Each time the clock plays, its hours, its speed and its shape are chosen anew.

Settings

Catalogs

A catalog says what a storage holds, so that the installation need not read every animation at its start or when the card goes in. A rebuild reads every animation of the storage whole and writes its catalog anew.

Reading the catalogs…

The installation cannot write this card; a computer can make its catalog.

Mapping

Each three-column snake has the board's data pin that drives it beside it. Viewed from the front, snake 1 is columns 1–3 at the left; each snake starts at the bottom and runs up, down, up.

Reading mapping…

From a photo

One photo of the wall with the diagonal showing tells which snake each position drives: the app reads it and offers the mapping that puts every position right. A snake that does not light is put where a place remains.

    The photo fits more than one reading. Only the wall tells them apart: which edge the wires come in at, which end strip is dark, and after Apply whether the diagonal begins at the bottom left.

    To check the wiring, turn on Show test animations and use the Tests group of Animations: the diagonal and one test for each snake, through the saved mapping. Only a person looking at the LEDs can judge them.

    Current limits

    A budget is a ceiling, in mA, on WLED's estimate of the current the LEDs draw; the estimates beside the fields are that estimate, not a measurement. A picture estimated above a budget is dimmed until it is within it, the wall as a whole or one snake. An empty field is no limit. A low wall budget also dims the diagonal that the mapping from a photo reads.

    Reading the current limits…

    No budgets are saved: the wall's budget is the installation's default.
