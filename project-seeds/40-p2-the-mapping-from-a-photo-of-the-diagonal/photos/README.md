# Photos of the installation, 2026-10-25

The owner's photos of the LED wall on its table, unpowered, from four sides, and two
screenshots of the app with the owner's first photo of the diagonal, for the tests of this
seed and for anyone who works on it: what a phone sees of 60 strips of 60
LEDs on a wooden board, 1.2 m across, with the connectors of the twenty snakes along one
edge. That edge, where the wires come out, is the bottom of the canvas: every snake starts
there and runs away from it, back, and away again. Each file is the owner's photo scaled
to 2,000 pixels across; the originals, 4,000 x 2,252, are on the share and on Drive under
`artifacts/odrig/photos/board-2026-10-25/`.

| File | Where the camera stood |
|---|---|
| `01-from-the-wires-edge-flash.jpg` | At the wires' edge, looking across the board; the flash lights every LED's lens, which is what a dark picture of unlit LEDs must not be taken for. |
| `02-from-the-side-turned.jpg` | At the board's side, the phone turned: the wires' edge is at the bottom of the picture, the board's far edge, with black tape, at the top. |
| `03-from-the-far-edge.jpg` | At the far edge, looking back towards the wires: the picture is upside down with respect to the canvas. |
| `04-from-above-the-wires-edge.jpg` | Above the wires' edge, the board filling the frame: the black tape of the far edge at the bottom of the picture. |
| `05-the-diagonal-from-the-side-screenshot.jpg` | The owner's screenshot of the app showing their first photo of the diagonal, cropped to the picture: the wall lit by the room, seen from its side with the wires' edge at the left, the lens of every unlit LED catching light. The first picture the analysis refused, and the one it must read: 57 lit LEDs, position 1 at the top of the picture and snake 1 at its left. |
| `06-the-diagonal-from-the-side-darkened-screenshot.jpg` | The same photo darkened by the owner on the phone, as a screenshot of the app: the dimmer LEDs are lost, and what is read agrees with 05. |

Photos 01 to 04 show no diagonal: with these the analysis must refuse. Photos 05 and 06
show it in a lit room: with these it must read.
