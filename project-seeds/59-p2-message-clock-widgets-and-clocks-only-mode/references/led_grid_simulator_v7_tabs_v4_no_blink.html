Clock

Minute and hour digits can roll randomly at the same time as seconds. Every wheel uses the same scroll animation.

Pendulum

1.0 = physical harmonic motion, smooth reversal at edges. Higher = sharper center crossing, still no dwell/stop.

Color wave

LED diameter 0.3 cm · pitch 1.6 cm. The last three rows contain the white moving square. Text remains small and bright white at one fixed size. Countdown zero stays visible during three follow-up sentences. The seconds roll normally; minute/hour wheels may roll independently. Text tabs restart the selected sequence. The motion scene slows the clock and pendulum together; once stopped, digits remain frozen and the clock and pendulum stay steadily lit.
