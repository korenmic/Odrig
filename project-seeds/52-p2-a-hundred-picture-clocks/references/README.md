- `original/`: the read-only extraction, made on 2026-10-28 from the owner's logged-in
  browser, of the six demos of the conversation "רעיונות לאנימציות זמן"
  (https://chatgpt.com/share/6ae1379a-9dc8-83ed-8fee-9aca58017cc7), as its `README.txt`
  and `provenance.json` say; `animation.js` is the demos' own script. The six GIFs
  `original-*.gif` were rendered from that script, 60 x 60, 100 ms a frame, one period
  of each demo, in a headless Chrome with a profile of its own.
- `standalone/`: the six GIFs a standalone page held, 60 x 60, 18 seconds each, and that
  page's script, `standalone-script.js`, which draws the same six ideas its own way.
- `conversation.txt`: the text of the conversation.
